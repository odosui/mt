# Flashcards

There is quite a lot of research showing that flashcards at [spaced intervals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaced_repetition) are one of the most effective ways to learn and retain information. This is why it's one of the core features of `mt`.

Anyone familiar with flashcards software like [Anki](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anki_(software)>) or [SuperMemo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperMemo) should feel right at home with `mt`'s flashcards.

The only difference is that flashcards are always assigned to a particular note. The flashcards panel can be accessed by clicking the flashcards button floating on the right side of the note editor.

![Flashcards panel](4__cards.png)

Click the "+" icon to add a new flashcard. When learning a new language, sometimes it's convenient to add both cards (there is a checkbox for that).

![New card form](4__flashcards_form.png)

Similarly to notes, the flashcards review schedule is managed by `mt`. The algorithm is loosely based on the **SM-2 algorithm** with a few tweaks. When you review a flashcard, there are just two options: "Again" and "Good". If you click "Again", the entire schedule will be reset. If you click "Good", the next review will be scheduled.

![Reviewing](4__flashcard_review.png)
